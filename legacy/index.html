05 — PROCESS

IDEA →
PRODUCT

Seven movements, one direction. Typography marks the descent from ambiguity to a living, evolving product.

PHASE01

Discover

Immersion: audience, market, constraints, intent. We listen harder than we sketch — the sharpest insights arrive before a single pixel.

→
PHASE02

Define

Thesis and architecture: positioning, scope, information hierarchy, success criteria. Ambiguity converted into a buildable map.

→
PHASE03

Design

Art direction and interface: composition, typography, imagery, design tokens. Beauty drafted against purpose, never decoration.

→
PHASE04

Build

Creative engineering: responsive front-end, interaction systems, performance budgets. The design becomes software that holds up.

→
PHASE05

Refine

Motion tuning, edge cases, accessibility, device sweeps from 320 to 1920. Detail is where distinction is manufactured.

→
PHASE06

Launch

Release choreography: hardening, analytics, handover. A calm, deliberate entry into the world — never a scramble.

→
PHASE07

Evolve

Measure, learn, extend. Products are conversations — we stay to sharpen what launch taught us and compound the advantage.
